Meeting Planner Launcher

Add up to five locations, resolve their timezones, and open timeanddate.com Meeting Planner with everyone pre-filled.

Locations

Enter at least two locations. Empty fields are skipped.

Geocoding via Photon (OpenStreetMap). Timezones via TimeAPI.io. Planner opens on timeanddate.com with the nearest listed city in the same timezone when an exact match isn’t available.
